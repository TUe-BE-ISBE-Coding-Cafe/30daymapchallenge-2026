> First fill in the **[sign-up form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/Qqk5yjSuVT)** so we can send you updates, even if you
> can't come to the café. This table is just for spreading the days.

an organiser to add you. Several people per day is fine – separate names with a comma.
